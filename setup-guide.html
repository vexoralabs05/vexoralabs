Mobitel IP Hunting Guide

Because Mobitel uses dynamic IP subnets, their Zoom and promotional packages require acquiring a specific gateway IP address for the Singapore tunnel to unlock full uncapped speed. Here is how to do it in 2 minutes:

Step 1

Check Your Current IP

Open NetMod Syna on Android. Look at the IP address shown at the bottom or open the IP Hunter tab.

Step 2

Toggle Airplane Mode

Turn Airplane Mode ON for 3 seconds, then turn it OFF. Mobitel will assign your SIM a new internal IP.

Step 3

Target Acquired & Connect

Once IP matches the target range specified in your config message, hit START. Full speeds are unlocked!
