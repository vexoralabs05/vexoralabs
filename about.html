Our Story & Purpose

Breaking Digital Barriers in Sri Lanka

In Sri Lanka, internet service providers often restrict speeds, enforce restrictive sub-buckets (like Zoom-only or YouTube-only packages), and throttle bandwidth. Meanwhile, global tech services like Google Gemini AI and Antigravity IDE frequently display the dreaded "This account is ineligible because it is not currently available in your location" error.

Vexora Labs was created to solve this problem. By operating private, low-latency tunneling nodes in Singapore and offering genuine, student-affordable software licenses (Canva Pro, Office 2024, Windows 11 Pro), we enable every Sri Lankan to work, create, game, and learn without limits.
